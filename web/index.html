Privacy Charter & Laws

Safe Spaces AI Innovation Hub - Data Sovereignty

1. Local-First Default Retention

Safe Spaces Grafana is engineered from the ground up to keep your personal data, meeting logs, chat transcripts, and configurations exclusively on your local device. We run zero centralized databases and zero behavioral tracking.

2. Voluntary Cloud Sync (filefran.safespaceshub.com)

Data is only uploaded to our cloud infrastructure if you explicitly toggle voluntary cloud backup in your settings. When enabled, encrypted archives are stored in our dedicated vault at https://filefran.safespaceshub.com. You retain complete ownership and can purge remote copies at any time.

3. Multi-Party Consensus Recording

No meeting can ever be recorded without the unanimous, verifiable consent of all connected participants. Any participant has the sovereign right to veto or revoke recording mid-call, halting capture instantly.

4. African and International Privacy Compliance

Our operations strictly adhere to the Uganda Data Protection and Privacy Act 2019, Kenya Data Protection Act 2019, the African Union Convention on Cyber Security and Personal Data Protection (Malabo Convention), and the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

5. Rights of the Data Subject

  • Right to Portability: Export all locally held data as JSON via the Device Storage menu.
  • Right to Erasure: One-click local storage purge eliminates all credentials and logs instantly.
  • Right to Rectification: Edit your display name, email, and preferences at any time.

Cookie & Storage Policy

Functional local tokens only • Zero tracking

Strictly Functional Storage

Safe Spaces Grafana does not use advertising, marketing, or behavioral tracking cookies.

We use isolated browser local storage strictly for functional application operations:

  • grafana_token: Cryptographic session token for authenticated hosts to dispatch email invitations via mail.safespaceshub.com.
  • grafana_profile: Locally saved display name and avatar choices.
  • grafana_settings: Audio, video, and auto-admit preferences.
  • grafana_sync_pref: Your voluntary consent flag for backups to filefran.safespaceshub.com.

Zero Third-Party Disclosures

No local storage tokens or cookies are ever transferred to advertising networks, search engines, or analytics services.
